The runtime uses the upstream Pilot_prompt.md verbatim, followed by the shared
IN/OUT interface and task requirement. Generated PySpice is executed in the
common no-network sandbox and its raw deck is scored by the frozen evaluator.
This is an adapted prompt-method comparison, not an unmodified official runner.
